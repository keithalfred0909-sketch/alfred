> Market intraday momentum (Gao, Han, Li & Zhou, Journal of Financial Economics, 2018, documented on the S&P 500 ETF): the return from the previous trading day's 16:00 New York close to 10:00 New York predicts the sign of the last half-hour return (15:30-16:00). Long the last half hour after a positive first-half-hour return; short after a negative one. One trade per trading day.

Rationale: Published, economically motivated effect (late informed trading / hedging flows into the close). Applied here to the Nasdaq 100 (Dukascopy USATECHIDXUSD CFD) as an EXTENSION of the S&P 500 result, on 2017-2026 data. Entry at the 15:30 bar open, exit at the 16:00 bar close (hold 0 = exit at the close of the entry bar). Costs: NQ-futures-like ~0.87 bps per round trip (config NAS100_M30); CFD costs reported separately as a sensitivity, not as the verdict. Known conservative biases of the standard battery here: the parameter-perturbation neighbours hold into the 16:00-16:30 after-hours bar, which is outside the hypothesis.

Variants fixed in advance (2):

1. LONG when im_open_sign == 1 AND tod == 930; exit at the close of the entry bar
